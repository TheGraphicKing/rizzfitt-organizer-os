# Prompt 11 - Player microsite

**Goal:** the public event page players open from WhatsApp: beautiful in the organizer's accent colour, registration in about 2 minutes, live scores that feel like a sports app.

**Read again:** `design/11` P-01 to P-03, `design/10` F13, `design/07` S8, S11, S15, `design/06` event accent rules.

## Build
1. **P-01 Event home:** S15 hero themed by event accent and cover style, status pill, categories with price and spots left, about, schedule, live now, sponsors, venue, FAQ, organizer WhatsApp, sticky Register button.
2. **P-02 Register:** category, you (mobile lookup with returning-player autofill), partner (lookup or WhatsApp invite), fields chosen by the organizer, consent, mock UPI payment sheet (or pay at desk), ticket-style confirmation with QR, calendar and WhatsApp group buttons, confetti.
3. **P-03 Live and me:** live score bugs synced with the umpire app, schedule by court or category, draw and S8 bracket, standings, Me (enter mobile, see next match, court and opponent).
4. Mobile-first at 390 px; desktop shows a centered column with the hero wide.

## Acceptance
- [ ] Every seeded event's microsite looks distinct through its accent and cover style, with text contrast checked.
- [ ] A registration made here appears instantly in the organizer's Registrations, Home and Money.
- [ ] Live tab updates while an umpire scores in another tab.
- [ ] Entire flow works with keyboard and screen reader labels.
