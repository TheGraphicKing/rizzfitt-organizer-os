# Prompt 09 - Messages, Results, Money, Sponsors & screens, Launch kit, Settings

**Goal:** everything around the matches: talking to players, results and exports, money and payouts, sponsor screens, the launch kit with a 3D poster studio, and Settings.

**Read again:** `design/11` W-10 to W-15, `design/10` F11, F12, `design/07` S11, S14, `docs/02` sections 4.8, 4.13, 4.14, 4.20, 4.21.

## Build
1. **W-10 Messages:** list with stats, composer (channel, audience builder with live count and missing-email note, templates, variables, per-channel preview, send now or schedule), simulated delivery stats.
2. **W-11 Results:** S11 podium per finished category, standings and bracket, certificates (mock), DUPR sync panel with plain reasons and retry, reports grid with all current exports (generate mock CSV files).
3. **W-12 Money:** summary tiles with a single fees total (no rates), collections chart, online vs desk split, by category, payouts timeline, payments with refund (confirm), invoices, payout account card.
4. **W-13 Sponsors & screens:** sponsors with drag order, court TV designer with live scaled preview of T-01, open-on-screen URL and QR, media playlist.
5. **W-14 Launch kit:** event page editor-lite with phone and desktop preview; S14 poster studio (templates, editable text, drag-to-rotate 3D, size presets, Download PNG); message templates.
6. **W-15 Settings:** every setup section reusing wizard components, team members and roles (Owner, Manager, Desk, Court captain with courts), Advanced (ranking editor, timeouts by stage, per-category rule overrides, form fields), Danger zone with confirms (type-to-confirm only for cancelling an event).

## Acceptance
- [ ] Posters download as real PNG files with the event name and accent.
- [ ] Money never shows platform fee rates; totals reconcile with Registrations.
- [ ] Every setting from setup can be changed in Settings and the app updates (for example turning off court TV removes Sponsors & screens' TV section and the TV tab in the switcher).
- [ ] Co-organizer roles change what that persona can see.
